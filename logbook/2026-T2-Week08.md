**Date:** 12–14 Jun 2026

**What I worked on:** Added a "simple" navigation mode alongside the existing skeleton/graph-based one, selectable via a `navigation_type` config parameter. It thresholds the black line and green markers separately, weights and combines them, takes the largest contour's centroid (`cv::moments`), blends in the green centroid when it's large enough, and converts the offset into a steering error via `atan`. Also added raw video recording (`cv::VideoWriter`) for later review.

**Reflection:** Realising a config-selectable "simple" colour-threshold mode could sit alongside the full graph pipeline gave a fallback that's much faster to tune under time pressure than debugging the skeleton/graph approach live.

**Decisions made:** Made navigation type a runtime parameter rather than a compile-time choice, and used `cv::moments` on the largest contour for a fast, well-understood centroid calculation for the simple path rather than reusing the heavier skeleton-graph extraction.

**Testing notes:** Recorded raw camera footage to a video file during runs so lighting/threshold issues could be reviewed afterwards rather than only observed live.
